## 📲 How to Install as an App on Phones

### iPhone (iOS Safari)
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow pointing up `⎙`) at the bottom of Safari.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (`⊞`).
4. Tap **Add**. The app icon will appear on your iPhone home screen and open in full-screen mode just like a native App Store app!

### Android (Google Chrome)
1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three dots menu (**⋮**) in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. The app will install directly to your app drawer and home screen.
